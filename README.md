# Slide Clock

An installable web app for running a talk from your phone.

Tell it how many slides you have and how long you have to speak, write a line against each slide,
and then present from a card per slide: a ring counting down the time for that slide, your note at a
size you can read at arm's length, what is coming next, and how far behind or ahead you are running.

**Open it:** https://pohsieh.github.io/slide-clock/

On an iPhone, tap **Share** then **Add to Home Screen** and it behaves like any other app — its own
icon, full screen, and it works with no network, which matters in a room whose wifi you do not
trust. Nothing is uploaded; everything stays in the browser on your own device.

## Why a countdown per slide

A single timer for the whole talk tells you nothing useful until it is too late. A countdown per
slide tells you where you are, and the pace figure tells you the thing that actually causes
overruns: how much time you already owe from the slides behind you.
